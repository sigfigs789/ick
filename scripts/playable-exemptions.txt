# Words held playable despite carrying a rejection reason, one per line.
#
# The derived rejections (src/data/derived-rejections.json) come from Wiktionary's
# own sense labels, applied in bulk: a word every ordinary sense of which is
# obsolete, archaic or dated -- or that has no ordinary sense at all, because it is
# an abbreviation or a proper noun -- is not playable, and the player is told which.
# A word listed here is kept out of that file and stays playable, and a refresh
# will not put it back. Use it when the label is right but the word is still a good
# find; use scripts/blocklist.txt for the opposite case.
