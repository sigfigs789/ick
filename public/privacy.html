Privacy Policy

WordInk — last updated 24 October 2026

WordInk is a daily word game, run by one person. This page describes what the game stores and why, in plain terms. It covers both the website at wordink.app and the WordInk app on Reddit.

There are no accounts on the website

The website asks for nothing. There is no sign-up, no email address, no password, and nothing you type about yourself. Your browser invents a random identifier the first time you play, and that identifier is what a leaderboard row belongs to. The name shown beside it — something like StormyFox47 — is computed from that identifier. It is not a name you choose and it says nothing about you.

Clearing your browser's site data erases that identifier, your puzzle progress and your streak. There is no way to recover them, because there is nothing tying them to you.

What the website stores

  • In your browser: your random identifier, the puzzles you have played, your streak, and which notices you have seen. This stays on your device.
  • On the server: one row per player per puzzle — the random identifier, the date, your rarest word of that day, and your scores. This is what the leaderboard is built from.
  • Words looked up: when you try a word the game's dictionary does not carry, the word is checked against a dictionary service and the answer is recorded so it need not be asked again. The record is of the word, not of who tried it.
  • Chat: a message you post is stored with your random identifier and handle, and shown to everyone in that puzzle's chat under the handle only. Reports you make are stored with your identifier so the same message is not counted twice. Players you block are remembered on your device alone.
  • Usage events: anonymous counts of things like puzzles started and words accepted, used to see whether the game works. No identifier is attached.

The Reddit app

Inside Reddit, the game runs as a Reddit app. There, your Reddit username is your identity — Reddit tells the app who you are, and your scores are stored under that username on Reddit's own infrastructure. Your username is displayed on the leaderboard inside the app, as u/yourname.

  • Your Reddit username is not sent off Reddit, and is not stored in any database outside it.
  • The Reddit app reports no usage analytics at all.
  • It reads public leaderboard totals and word data from the website's database so that both sets of players can appear on one board. That is a one-way read: nothing about Reddit players is written back.

What is never collected

No names, no email addresses, no passwords, no payment details, no location, no contacts, and no advertising or cross-site tracking identifiers. The game does not sell data, and there is nothing here that could be sold.

Who else sees it

The website's data is held in a hosted database (Supabase) and served through a hosting provider (Vercel), both acting only as infrastructure. Dictionary lookups go to Merriam-Webster and Datamuse, and carry only the word being looked up. Reddit holds the data belonging to the Reddit app. Nobody else receives any of it.

Removing your data

On the website: clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device and disconnects you from your leaderboard rows, which cannot then be traced back to you. To have leaderboard rows deleted outright, or to ask anything about this policy, write to sigfigs789@gmail.com.

Children

WordInk is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the game itself.
