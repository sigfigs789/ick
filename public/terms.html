Scores and the words players find are shown publicly inside the game. On the website a player appears as a generated handle; in the Reddit app a player appears as their Reddit username. The word list

Which words the game accepts, and what each is worth, are judgements made by the game. They are revised as the dictionary is corrected, so a word's score can change and a word the game once accepted may later be rejected. This is part of how the game works rather than a fault in it. Word rarity is derived from published frequency data; dictionary lookups use Merriam-Webster and Datamuse.

The word list is built from SCOWL and from Wiktionary, whose content is used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Chat

Each day's puzzle has a chat room, on the website and in the WordInk app, where players appear under their generated handle. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive players. In the chat you must not post harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, spam, or anybody's personal details, yours included.

  • Messages are checked for profanity before they are posted.
  • Hold or tap a message to report it, or to block whoever wrote it so you no longer see their messages.
  • A message reported by several players is removed automatically. Reports are reviewed, normally within 24 hours, and a player who breaks these rules is removed from the chat.
  • To report anything else, write to the address under Contact.
