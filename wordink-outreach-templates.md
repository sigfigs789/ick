# WordInk outreach templates

Rule for all of these: **under 150 words, one link, one ask.** No press release, no
attachments on a first email. Subject lines are lowercase-plain on purpose — they
read like a person, not a campaign.

Replace `[NAME]` and the bracketed bits. Never send one unedited; one specific
sentence about *their* publication is what separates this from spam.

---

## 8. Show HN (self-post)

> **Title:** Show HN: WordInk – a daily word game scored by how rare your word is
>
> First comment:
>
> Every day there's a 2–3 letter drop; you submit words containing it as a
> contiguous substring. Instead of win/lose, each word gets a rarity score from
> Zipf frequency data (wordfreq), bucketed Very Common → Extremely Rare.
>
> The interesting problem was scoring fairness: a plural or a third-person form
> shouldn't out-score the word it came from, and a word no corpus attests isn't
> automatically a brilliant find — it's usually a misspelling or something archaic.
> Most of the work went into that.
>
> React + TypeScript, no backend, no accounts. The daily drop is derived
> deterministically from the date so everyone gets the same one.
